A simple video repurposing process

  • Start with a video file or inspect a supported public source URL.
  • Set the content type, desired number of clips, duration range and aspect ratio.
  • Generate, review and download the resulting clips. You remain in control of the final selection.

Supported creator formats

  • Choose 9:16 for vertical short-form content, 1:1 for square posts or 16:9 for landscape clips.
  • Use caption styles to make speech easier to follow.
  • Export MP4 files and make any final edits in your preferred publishing tool.

Frequently asked questions

How long can my source video be?

The practical limit depends on your deployment's upload-size setting, available memory, processing time and source format.

Yes. Set minimum and maximum clip duration in the creator studio before analysis.

Will every suggested moment be perfect?

No. AI suggestions need human review for context, accuracy and editorial quality.
