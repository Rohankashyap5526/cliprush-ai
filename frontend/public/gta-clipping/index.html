What the AI can—and cannot—detect

The current ClipRush AI highlight workflow is primarily based on speech transcription and transcript analysis. It can suggest moments around spoken reactions or commentary, but it does not reliably detect visual events such as GTA kills, police chases, stunts, races, missions or funny silent gameplay. For footage without speech, review the recording and select the timestamps you want to keep using your editing workflow. Always check the final clip for context and accuracy.
