Turn long recordings into short-form videos. ClipRush AI helps you transcribe a source video, identify promising moments and render clips with timed captions.

ClipRush AI is a creator tool for turning longer video recordings into shorter, shareable edits. Start with a video file or a supported public source URL, choose your clip preferences, and let the Groq-powered workflow help identify moments worth reviewing.

How the AI clipping workflow works

  • Add a video file or inspect a supported YouTube, Twitch or Kick URL. Only use content you own or are authorized to process.
  • ClipRush sends the audio for speech transcription and uses an AI model to select candidate highlight moments.
  • Choose clip count, duration range, aspect ratio and caption style, then render and review the generated MP4 clips.

Designed for short-form workflows

  • Create vertical 9:16 outputs for Shorts and Reels-style feeds, or choose square and landscape formats.
  • Use timed captions to make spoken content easier to follow.
  • Review each generated clip and download the MP4 when it is ready.

Frequently asked questions

What does ClipRush AI do?

It helps turn longer videos into shorter clips by transcribing speech, selecting candidate highlights and rendering clips with captions.

Can I upload my own video?

Yes. The creator studio supports video uploads, subject to the configured upload-size limit and supported video formats.

Does AI guarantee viral clips?

No. AI-selected moments are suggestions, not a guarantee of views, engagement or virality. Review clips before publishing.
