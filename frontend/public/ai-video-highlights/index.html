AI video highlights generator

Find candidate moments in longer videos and turn selected highlights into short, captioned clips.

ClipRush AI combines speech transcription with AI-assisted moment selection to help creators locate interesting sections in a longer recording. It then renders selected ranges as individual videos with timed captions, ready for review and download.

How highlight selection works

  • Groq Whisper transcribes the source audio and provides word-level timing information.
  • The configured Groq chat model evaluates transcript segments and proposes candidate highlight ranges.
  • ClipRush renders the selected ranges with your chosen aspect ratio and caption style.

Review before you share

  • Check whether each excerpt preserves the original meaning and surrounding context.
  • Correct any caption or timing issues in your editing workflow if needed.
  • Choose highlights that are relevant to your audience rather than relying only on an automated score.

Frequently asked questions

Which AI provider does ClipRush use?

The current deployment is configured to use Groq for transcription and highlight selection.

Does the AI understand every visual scene?

The current highlight-selection workflow is primarily transcript and speech driven; it should not be treated as full visual understanding.

Are highlights automatically posted online?

No. ClipRush generates downloadable clips; publishing is a separate step.
