Frequently asked questions

Does ClipRush publish directly to YouTube?

The current workflow generates downloadable MP4 clips. It does not automatically publish them to your YouTube channel.

Can I make vertical 9:16 clips?

Yes. Select 9:16 in the output format settings before generating clips.

Can I use any YouTube video?

Only process videos you own or have permission to use, and respect platform terms and copyright.
