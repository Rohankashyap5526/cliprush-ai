AI podcast clip maker

Turn podcast conversations and interviews into short video moments that are easier to review, share and repurpose.

Podcasts and interviews often contain useful insights spread across long recordings. ClipRush AI can transcribe spoken audio, select candidate moments and render short clips with timed captions, helping you prepare excerpts for your social content workflow.

From conversation to candidate clips

  • Upload a podcast video or an interview recording in a supported video format.
  • Choose Podcast / interview as the content type and configure the clip count and duration range.
  • Let the workflow transcribe speech and suggest moments, then review the context and captions before exporting.

Tips for better podcast clips

  • Use recordings with clear speech and minimal background noise.
  • Review the beginning and ending of each excerpt so the conversation makes sense out of context.
  • Check speaker names, captions and permissions before sharing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use audio-only podcasts?

The current creator workflow accepts video files. An audio-only file may need to be converted to a supported video format first.

Does ClipRush add captions?

Yes. The generated clips can include timed captions burned into the video.

Can it automatically publish clips?

No. The current workflow generates MP4 files for you to review and publish yourself.
